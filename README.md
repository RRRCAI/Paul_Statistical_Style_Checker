To memorise Paul, I made a Praat script for automatic statistical writing-style checking.

Over the last four years, Paul has carried out many strict checks on the statistical writing in our publications and in my dissertation. He has very specific requirements when it comes to statistical reporting and formatting. So, I went through his feedback over the years and summarised it into a few commands (or, perhaps more appropriately, “Paul’s rules”), and then built them into Praat.

In this way, if anyone wants Paul to do some editing in the future, they can simply run the script in Praat. It checks things such as p-values, t-values, odds ratios, significant digits, confidence intervals, and several other details that Paul would probably notice immediately.

I have already tested it on my dissertation — and unsurprisingly, “Paul” still found quite a few things to correct.
